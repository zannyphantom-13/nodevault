# NodeVault — Per-Tier Financial Breakdown (Updated)

## Formula Key

| Term | Formula | Meaning |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Reward Target** | F × 100% | Total gifts user receives to graduate |
| **Max Refundable Gross** | F × 70% | Tier A best case — zero gifts, end-of-month |
| **Max Refundable Net** | Gross × 97% | After 3% refund tax |
| **Protocol Margin Held** | F × 40% | Platform locks on entry — never returned |
| **Tier C Threshold** | F × 60% | Gifts reach this → refund disqualified |
| **Platform Total at Graduation** | F × 135% | Entry Fee kept (F) + 35% outbound fee on F in gifts withdrawn = F + 0.35F |

> **Platform Total at Graduation breakdown:**
> - `+F` → User's entry fee (100% retained — Tier C has no refund)
> - `+0.35F` → 35% outbound fee collected on the user's gift withdrawals
> - `= 1.35 × F` total platform revenue from one fully graduated user

## How Platform Total at Graduation is Calculated

```
USER: Pays Entry Fee (F)
  └─ Receives gifts over time = F total (graduation point)
  └─ On each withdrawal: pays 35% outbound fee

PLATFORM collects:
  ├─ [1] Entry Fee:         F        (fully retained — Tier C = no refund)
  └─ [2] Outbound Fees:     F × 0.35 (35% on all gift withdrawals)
                           ──────────
  Total Platform Revenue:   F × 1.35

Example — Echo Node NG (₦16,000):
  ₦16,000 (entry) + ₦5,600 (35% on ₦16,000 gifts) = ₦21,600 ✓

Example — Genesis Supreme NG (₦2,560,000):
  ₦2,560,000 + ₦896,000 = ₦3,456,000 ✓
```

| **Tier A** — Early Exit | 0 gifts received | 63% of F | −10% on gross | −3% | **61.1% of F** |
| **Tier B** — After 6 months | Gifts < 60% of F | 60% of F minus gifts received | None | −3% | Variable |
| **Tier B** — Before 6 months | Gifts < 60% of F | Locked | — | — | **₦0 / $0** |
| **Tier C** — Any time | Gifts ≥ 60% of F | Disqualified | — | — | **₦0 / $0** |
