# NodeVault — Per-Tier Financial Breakdown

## Formula Key
| Term | Formula | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| **Reward Target** | Entry Fee × 100% | Total gifts user must receive to graduate (Tier C threshold is 60%, full graduation = 100%) |
| **Max Refundable (Gross)** | Entry Fee × 70% | Tier A best case — zero gifts received, end-of-month exit |
| **Max Refundable (Net)** | Gross × 97% | After 3% refund tax deducted |
| **Fixed Held (Protocol Margin)** | Entry Fee × 40% | Platform locks this permanently on entry |
| **Tier C Threshold** | Entry Fee × 60% | Once gifts reach this, refund is disqualified |
| **Platform Profit at Graduation** | Entry Fee × 35% | Outbound fee (35%) collected when user withdraws their earned gifts |
| **Total Platform Revenue** | Protocol Margin + Outbound Fee | = 40% + 35% of gifts withdrawn = combined platform take |

> ⚠️ Note: The 35% outbound fee applies to withdrawals from gifts/yield — not from the entry fee itself. The 40% protocol margin is locked from the entry fee. These are two separate revenue streams.

## Summary — What Each Party Gets

```
USER ENTRY FEE: F
├── 40% → Protocol Margin (fixed, held by platform from day one)
└── 60% → Available for pool redistribution

AT GRADUATION (user received gifts = F):
├── User withdraws gifts:   F (gross) → net after 35% outbound = 0.65F
├── Platform outbound fee:  0.35F collected on user's withdrawals
└── Platform total retained: 40% (margin) + 35% (outbound on gifts)

REFUND SCENARIOS (user exits early):
├── Tier A (0 gifts): Max refund = 70% of F → net 67.9% after 3% tax
├── Tier B (<60% gifts received, after 6 months): Top-up to 60% cap − gifts received → net after 3% tax
└── Tier C (≥60% gifts received): ₦0 / $0 — disqualified
```
