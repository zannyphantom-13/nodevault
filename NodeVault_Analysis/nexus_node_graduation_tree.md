# NodeVault — Nexus Node Graduation: Binary Tree Structure & Accounting

## Overview

To graduate at the **Nexus Node (Tier 9 — ₦250,000)** level, a user must fill a complete **10-level binary power tree** of **1,023 members** beneath them.

---

## Binary Tree Structure (10 Levels)

```
Level 0 (Root — The Graduating User)
    1 person
    │
Level 1
    2 people
    │
Level 2
    4 people
    │
Level 3
    8 people
    │
Level 4
    16 people
    │
Level 5
    32 people
    │
Level 6
    64 people
    │
Level 7
    128 people
    │
Level 8
    256 people
    │
Level 9 (Base/Leaves)
    512 people
```

| Level | Members at Level | Cumulative Total | Member Type |
|-------|:---------------:|:----------------:|-------------|
| 0 (Root) | 1     | 1     | Graduating User |
| 1        | 2     | 3     | Qualifying (Gift Eligible) |
| 2        | 4     | 7     | Qualifying (Gift Eligible) |
| 3        | 8     | 15    | Qualifying (Gift Eligible) |
| 4        | 16    | 31    | Qualifying (Gift Eligible) |
| 5        | 32    | 63    | Qualifying (Gift Eligible) |
| 6        | 64    | 127   | Qualifying (Gift Eligible) |
| 7        | 128   | 255   | Qualifying (Gift Eligible) |
| 8        | 256   | 511   | Non-Recruiting (Leaf) |
| 9        | 512   | 1,023 | Non-Recruiting (Leaf) |

**Total tree members: 1,023**
- **255 qualifying/recruiting members** (Levels 0–7) → receive gift rewards
- **768 non-recruiting leaf members** (Levels 8–9) → receive 70% refund (₦175,000)

---

## Step-by-Step Accounting Summary

### 1. Total Cash Inflow (Revenue)
```
1,023 members × ₦250,000 entry fee = +₦255,750,000
```

### 2. Total Gift Rewards Paid Out (Expenses)
```
255 qualifying members (Levels 0–7) × ₦190,000 avg gifts = -₦48,450,000
                                                             ───────────────
Subtotal remaining:                                         +₦207,300,000
```

### 3. Total Refunds Paid Out (Liabilities)
```
768 non-recruiting members (Levels 8–9) × ₦175,000 (70% refund) = -₦134,400,000
```

### 4. Net Final Cash Remaining (Platform Profit per Graduation Event)
```
₦207,300,000 − ₦134,400,000 = +₦72,900,000
```

## Notes
- The **70% refund** for non-recruiting members is subject to a **3% refund tax**, making their actual net receipt ₦169,750 (not shown in the aggregate above — adjust if applying at scale).
- Levels 8–9 leaf members are in **Tier B/A refund territory** — they paid in but did not qualify for full gift rewards.
- The graduating root user (Level 0) receives their full gift reward and exits the protocol at graduation.
- This model applies to the **single global pool structure** of Nexus Node and above (Tiers 9–15).
