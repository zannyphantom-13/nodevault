## 3. Refund Eligibility Engine (Net Calculation Rules)

### Tier A — Full Protection (0% gifts received)
- **Gross Refund:** 70% of Entry Fee.
- **Timing:** Only accessible at the **end of the month**.
- **Penalty:** If withdrawn early (before the end of the month), a **10% early withdrawal fee** applies.
- **Tax:** A flat **3% tax** applies to the refund (no other taxes apply).

### Tier B — Partial Protection (Gifts received > 0, but < 60% of Entry Fee)
- **Gross Refund:** `(Entry Fee × 0.60) − Cumulative Gifts Received` (Tops up to 60%).
- **Timing:** Subject to a **6-Month Lock-in period**. No refunds permitted before 6 months.
- **Tax:** A flat **3% tax** applies to the refund.

### Tier C — Disqualified (Gifts received ≥ 60% of Entry Fee)
- **Gross Refund:** ₦0 / $0. Disqualified immediately upon meeting or exceeding the 60% cap.

  - Tier 6: 50 Genesis Groups
  - Tier 7: 40 Genesis Groups
  - Tier 8: 30 Genesis Groups
- **Load Balancing:** New unassigned members distributed across all active pools  
- **Founder Bid:** One-time boost bid for leaderboard placement  
- **Top-20 Leaderboard:** Highest 20 bids featured at top of marketplace  
- **Proof of Trust Metric:** "Total Bidding Stake Accumulated" displayed publicly  

### Tiers 9–15 (Nexus Node → Genesis Supreme): Single Global Pool
- **Max Active Pools:** Exactly **1 group per tier** — no competition, no bidding  
- **No Leaderboard:** Marketplace bidding engine does NOT apply at these tiers  
- **Auto-Join:** All stakers at these tiers automatically join the single global pool  
- **Exclusive Access:** Single-pool exclusivity signals premium tier status

## 6. Graduate Dividend System

- Completing full matrix progression → **Protocol Graduate Status**

### Nigeria Protocol (🇳🇬)
- Monthly dividend funded from institutional Treasury Bill returns (15–20% p.a.).
- **50% Penalty Rule:** If any downline member requests a refund, the graduate's dividend is halved that month.

### US Protocol (🇺🇸)
- **No ongoing dividends.** Once a US user completes the full matrix progression and reaches the graduation point, they exit the system entirely and are no longer entitled to any further yields or dividends.

## 10. Phase 2 Backend Architecture (High Scalability)

> [!NOTE]
> Phase 1 is fully frontend with mock data + local state. Because this platform must support **millions of concurrent users**, handle complex hierarchical referral trees, and manage real-time pool bidding, simple BaaS (like Firebase) is insufficient. Phase 2 will utilize a high-performance, distributed stack:

- **Compute:** Node.js or Go Microservices for independent scaling of the refund engine, bidding matrix, and tree updates.
- **Database (Relational):** PostgreSQL for ACID-compliant tracking of financial transactions, net unrecovered principal, and exact dividend disbursements.
- **Caching & Real-Time:** Redis for real-time leaderboard bidding (Tiers 1–8) and high-speed pool capacity checking.
- **Tree/Graph Data:** Optimized materialized paths in Postgres, or a dedicated Graph DB to quickly resolve deep Sub-Node relationships.
